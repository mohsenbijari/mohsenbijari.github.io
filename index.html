About Me

Research interests and academic background

I am a quantitative genetics researcher with experience in statistical modelling, animal breeding, and data-driven evaluation of health and performance traits in dairy cattle.

My research interests include longevity, welfare-related traits, and the integration of phenotypic big data with genetic improvement programs.

I have experience working with complex livestock datasets and applying SAS and R-based statistical workflows.

Research Projects

Selected academic and research projects

2017 – 2020

Economic Consequences of FMD

M.Sc. thesis at Isfahan University of Technology investigating the economic, productive and reproductive consequences of foot-and-mouth disease in Iranian Holstein cows.

  • Developed a new economic formula to estimate financial losses.
  • Investigated FMD occurrence across seasons and parity.
  • Studied productive and reproductive traits.
  • Used SQL for data management.
  • Statistical analysis using SAS and R.
2016

Dairy Cow Transition Period

B.Sc. project at Birjand University focusing on methods to evaluate the success of the transition period in early lactation of dairy cows.

  • Analysed longitudinal physiological and performance data.
  • Used mixed models and statistical prediction methods.
  • Explored welfare and metabolic traits related to longevity.
  • Managed complex phenotypic datasets.
2015

Freshwater Lobster Breeding

B.Sc. seminar at Birjand University on management and breeding of freshwater lobsters.

  • Reproductive cycles and larval stages.
  • Environmental requirements.
  • Water quality and feed management.
  • Growth and survival optimization.
