Research Article

Productivity, reproduction and economic losses due to foot-and-mouth disease (FMD) in Iranian Holstein cows

Authors: Mohsen Bijari, Ali Sadeghi-Sefidmazgi, Maryam Ansari-Lari, Morteza Hosseini Ghaffari

Preventive Veterinary Medicine · Volume 238 · 106471

Abstract

This retrospective study investigated the impact of foot-and-mouth disease (FMD) on productive and reproductive traits, economic losses and culling trends in Holstein cows in Iran. Data from nine industrial and semi-industrial dairy herds were analyzed over a decade (2009–2019), including 49,400 records from 20,330 cows. A mixed linear model was used to evaluate the effects of FMD on milk production, fat and protein yields and reproductive traits, while a simulated bioeconomic model was used to estimate financial losses.

Research Article

A Study on some Factors Affecting the Incidence of Foot and Mouth Disease and the Culling Associated with in Iranian Dairy Cows

Authors: Mohsen Bijari & Ali Sadeghi-Sefidmazgi

Iranian Journal of Animal Science · 54(3) · 289–301

Abstract

This study investigated the incidence, culling, abortion, stillbirth and weight loss associated with foot-and-mouth disease outbreaks in Iranian dairy cows and examined their interactions across different parity groups. The study used 49,400 records from 20,330 cows collected between 2009 and 2019 from nine industrial dairy farms. Statistical analyses included the FREQ and LOGISTIC procedures in SAS.
