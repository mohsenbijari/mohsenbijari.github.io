B.Sc. Seminar · 2015

Management and Breeding of Freshwater Lobsters

An academic seminar focused on freshwater lobster breeding, reproductive biology, larval development and husbandry management.

Project Overview

This B.Sc. seminar examined the management and breeding of freshwater lobsters, with emphasis on reproductive biology, larval development and the environmental conditions required for successful culture.

The work considered biological and management factors that can influence breeding success, growth and survival under culture conditions.

Management & Breeding Considerations

The seminar reviewed biological and husbandry considerations relevant to freshwater lobster production, including reproductive management, larval development, environmental conditions, water quality and feeding practices.

These factors were considered in relation to breeding success, growth and survival under controlled culture conditions.

Academic Contribution

The project provided an academic overview of freshwater lobster breeding and husbandry, connecting reproductive biology with practical management factors involved in successful aquaculture production.
