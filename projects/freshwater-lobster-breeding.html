B.Sc. Seminar · 2015

Management and Breeding of Freshwater Lobsters

Birjand University · B.Sc. Seminar

Supervisor: Dr. Mohsen Mojtahedi

Project Overview

This academic seminar examined the management and breeding of freshwater lobsters, with emphasis on reproductive biology, larval development and the environmental conditions required for successful culture.

Project Year 2015
Degree B.Sc. Seminar
Institution Birjand University
Research Area Aquaculture & Animal Sciences
Research Focus Breeding & Husbandry
Project Result Passed with 20 / 20

Research Question

Which biological and management factors are important for successful freshwater lobster breeding and culture, particularly in relation to reproduction, larval development, environmental conditions, growth and survival?

Study Framework

The seminar reviewed the biological and husbandry requirements associated with freshwater lobster production. Particular attention was given to the reproductive cycle, larval stages and environmental conditions that influence successful culture.

Organism Freshwater lobsters
Biological Focus Reproductive cycles
Development Larval stages
Environment Culture conditions
Water Management Water quality
Feeding Feed management

Research Components

  • Review of freshwater lobster reproductive cycles and breeding processes.
  • Examination of larval development and early developmental stages.
  • Investigation of environmental requirements for successful culture.
  • Evaluation of management factors affecting growth and survival.
  • Consideration of water quality management in culture systems.
  • Review of feeding and feed-management practices relevant to successful production.

Management & Breeding Considerations

Reproductive Management

Understanding reproductive cycles and breeding requirements relevant to successful production.

Larval Development

Consideration of larval stages and the conditions required during early development.

Culture Environment

Evaluation of environmental requirements and water-quality considerations for culture systems.

Growth & Survival

Consideration of management, feeding and culture factors associated with growth and survival.

Academic Contribution

The seminar provided an academic overview of freshwater lobster breeding and husbandry, connecting reproductive biology with practical management factors involved in successful aquaculture production.

Academic Relevance

This undergraduate seminar contributed to my early academic experience in animal sciences and expanded my understanding of animal breeding, reproduction, environmental management and production systems.
