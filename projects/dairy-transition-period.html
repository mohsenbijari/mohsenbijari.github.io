B.Sc. Research Project · 2016

A Study of Methods to Evaluate the Success of the Transition Period in Early Lactation of Dairy Cows

Birjand University · B.Sc. Research Project

Supervisor: Dr. Mohsen Mojtahedi

Project Overview

This research project investigated methods for evaluating the success of the transition period in early lactation of dairy cows. The project focused on physiological and performance-related indicators and their relevance to health, productivity and productive lifespan.

Project Year 2016
Degree B.Sc. Research Project
Institution Birjand University
Research Area Dairy Cattle & Animal Science
Research Focus Transition Period & Early Lactation
Project Result Passed with 20 / 20

Research Question

How can the success of the transition period in early lactation be evaluated using physiological and performance-related indicators, and how can these indicators contribute to understanding dairy cow health and productive lifespan?

Study Framework

The project examined the transition period as a critical stage in the productive cycle of dairy cows. The research considered longitudinal physiological and performance information to evaluate changes during the transition into early lactation.

Animal Dairy cows
Production Stage Transition to early lactation
Data Type Longitudinal physiological data
Performance Production-related traits
Health Welfare and metabolic traits
Long-term Outcome Productive lifespan

Research Components

  • Evaluation of methods for assessing the success of the transition period in dairy cows.
  • Analysis of longitudinal physiological and performance-related data.
  • Investigation of physiological indicators associated with the transition into early lactation.
  • Evaluation of welfare and metabolic traits in relation to dairy cow performance.
  • Investigation of relationships between transition-period characteristics and productive lifespan.
  • Interpretation of trait relationships for animal health and genetic improvement decisions.

Methods & Analytical Skills

Longitudinal Data Analysis

Analysis and interpretation of physiological and performance-related information collected across different stages of the production cycle.

Mixed Models

Application of mixed-model approaches for evaluating longitudinal and performance-related dairy-cow data.

Statistical Prediction

Statistical prediction and interpretation of relationships among physiological and performance indicators.

Trait Interpretation

Evaluation of welfare, metabolic and productive traits in relation to health and productive lifespan.

Research Contribution

The project connected transition-period evaluation with dairy cow health, performance and productive lifespan. By considering physiological and performance-related traits together, the research provided a framework for interpreting transition-period success in relation to longer-term production outcomes.

Academic Relevance

This undergraduate research project contributed to my early research experience in dairy cattle, statistical analysis and the interpretation of complex animal performance data. It also established an early connection between animal health, production traits and productive lifespan that later became relevant to my research in quantitative genetics and animal health.
