M.Sc. Research Project · 2017–2020

A Study on Economic Consequences of Foot-and-Mouth Disease (FMD) in Holstein Cows in Iran

Isfahan University of Technology · M.Sc. Thesis

Supervisor: Dr. Ali Sadeghi-Sefidmazgi

Project Overview

This research project investigated the epidemiological, productive, reproductive and economic consequences of foot-and-mouth disease (FMD) in Holstein dairy cows in Iran. The project formed the basis of my M.Sc. thesis in Animal Genetics and Inbreeding at Isfahan University of Technology.

Project Period 2017–2020
Degree M.Sc. Research Thesis
Institution Isfahan University of Technology
Research Area Quantitative Genetics & Animal Health
Data Management SQL
Statistical Analysis SAS & R

Research Question

How does foot-and-mouth disease affect the productive, reproductive and economic performance of Iranian Holstein dairy cows, and which animal and management factors are associated with disease occurrence and its consequences?

Data & Study Framework

The research used large-scale dairy-cow records to investigate disease occurrence and its relationships with production, reproduction and animal-level characteristics. The analytical framework combined epidemiological investigation, statistical modelling and economic evaluation.

Animal Holstein dairy cows
Study Period 2009–2019
Records 49,400 records
Cows 20,330 cows
Herds 9 dairy herds
Data Management SQL-based workflow

Research Components

  • Development of a new economic formula for estimating financial losses caused by FMD.
  • Epidemiological investigation of FMD occurrence.
  • Investigation of disease occurrence across different seasons and parity groups.
  • Investigation of relationships between productive and reproductive traits and disease incidence.
  • Evaluation of the effects of FMD outbreaks on productive and reproductive traits.
  • Investigation of potential implications of disease, production and reproduction for longevity in Holstein cows.

Methods & Analytical Skills

Data Management

SQL-based management and preparation of complex dairy-cow datasets.

Statistical Analysis

Statistical analysis using SAS and R to investigate disease occurrence and production-related traits.

Epidemiological Analysis

Evaluation of disease occurrence according to season, parity and animal characteristics.

Economic Modelling

Development of an economic framework for estimating the financial consequences of FMD.

Research Contribution

The project integrated animal health, quantitative analysis, dairy production, reproduction and economic evaluation within a single research framework. A particular focus was placed on translating disease effects into measurable production, reproductive and financial consequences, while also investigating factors associated with disease occurrence.
